## Slide 15: Pre-Launch Strategy
**The Campaign: 9 to 12-Part Documentary Series**
* **The Cast:** Direct, interview-style features with the people who built the project—specifically the architects, designers, and painters.
* **The Execution:** Content designed to make people deeply inquisitive about what the page and posts are actually about.
* **The Hook:** Focus entirely on the creator's raw process, intentionally withholding the final space to leave the viewer curious about the end result of their work.

## Slide 16: The Launch
**The Realization: Providing Closure**
* **The Compilation:** Staging the ultimate payoff by compiling the final physical results alongside the genuine, raw reactions of the interviewed architects, designers, and painters.
* **The Delivery:** Transitioning from individual teasers to a single cohesive show put on for the audience to fully view and enjoy.
* **The Objective:** Resolve the pre-launch curiosity by matching the faces to the finished sanctuary.

## Slide 17: Post-Launch Strategy
**The Creator Relay**
* **Distribution:** One distinct vlog/pov type reel posted directly on each influencer’s individual page.
* **The Narrative Chain:** A continuous property tour where each creator's video starts exactly where the previous creator left off, allowing the journey to seamlessly continue.
* **The Mechanic:** Every piece of content is sequentially connected using the "link reel" feature, systematically increasing the hype and theatrics around the presence of our brand.

## Slide 18: Sustenance Strategy
**Retaining Attention: Immersive POV**
* **The Objective:** Retain the massive attention gained from the launch campaigns by shifting to content that continuously fascinates the audience.
* **The Execution:** A dedicated series of POV videos capturing the team behind the scenes, pulling the viewer directly into the hotel's daily motion.
* **The Specifics:** Authentic, first-person action shots tracking staff execution—specifically room service working, the chef cooking, and the bartender shaking glasses.

## Slide 19: The Pillars of Sustenance
**The 4 Core Pillars**
* **The Space as Character:** Presenting physical spaces as living, dynamic beings—almost like a matriarch. *Example: Tracking how the natural light in the lobby shifts throughout the day.*
* **The New Luxury:** Rejecting performative luxury to focus entirely on genuine service. *The Copy Rule: Connect with the audience using short, deliberate sentences.*
* **The Guest Beyond Hotel:** Using highly relatable visuals and settings that mirror the target audience's daily lives, ensuring they feel the brand completely understands their lifestyle and choices.
* **The Good Friction:** Focusing on analog elements completely removed from technology. *The Tactile Triggers: Using physical things that can be felt—like rain, water, fire, soil, flowers, towels, and handles—to evoke something deeply abstract.*

## Slide 20: Influencer Marketing Strategy
**The Alignment Guardrails**
* **The Persona:** Partner exclusively with culturally significant travel and lifestyle influencers who naturally embody our actual target audience.
* **The Filter:** Establish a strict matrix of "Yes’s and No’s" regarding the creators' archetypes and their existing audience profiles to protect brand positioning.
* **The Mandate:** Every collaboration must feel like a genuine, organic endorsement. Content must be entirely non-transactional in tone and execution.

## Slide 21: Performance Marketing
**The Paid Media Engine**
* **Meta Ads Framework:** Driven by precise audience targeting logic and a distinct creative approach, structured systematically across the launch and post-launch phases.
* **Google Ads Framework:** Clean, intent-driven search campaigns built around three core pillars: brand protection, category targeting, and strategic competitor conquest.
* **The Creative Fuel:** Directly amplifying the content generated by the influencers who mirror our exact target audience to maximize authenticity.

## Slide 22: Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
**Measuring True Luxury Resonance**
* **The Primary Metrics:** Success is measured strictly through **Saves and Shares** to build a deeply engaged, high-intent community.
* **The Top-Funnel Engine:** **Reach and Views** define our scale, acting as the filter that naturally guides the right audience to Altara.
* **The Core Philosophy:** Digital channels are positioned entirely to command absolute **consideration**. We focus on capturing the exact demographic that actively engages with us.
