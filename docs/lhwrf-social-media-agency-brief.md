# LHWRF Social Media Agency Brief

**Date**: 2026-05-06  
**Organisation**: Lupin Human Welfare & Research Foundation (LHWRF)  
**Context**: Summary of LHWRF, what the organisation appears to need from a social media agency, and the strategic direction for LinkedIn.

## Organisation Summary

Lupin Human Welfare & Research Foundation (LHWRF) is the social responsibility arm of Lupin Limited. It was founded by Dr. Desh Bandhu Gupta in 1988 and has worked for more than three decades across underserved rural regions in India.

LHWRF positions its work around rural development, sustainable livelihoods, healthcare access, and family-level poverty reduction. Its own description emphasizes a family-centered approach aimed at breaking the cycle of poverty in underdeveloped regions.

According to LHWRF's official website, the foundation has impacted more than 2.02 million beneficiaries across more than 5,400 villages in 26 districts across eight states. Its outreach page separately lists programmatic presence in 5,431 villages, 88 blocks, 23 districts, and nine states.

The foundation works with government, non-government partners, international development agencies, funders, and philanthropic organisations to reach underserved populations, including landless, tribal, scheduled caste, and minority communities.

## Core Program Areas

### Lives

The Lives program focuses on community health and improved access to healthcare services. Key areas include:

- Non-communicable diseases
- Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
- Cardiovascular Diseases (CVDs)
- Asthma
- Hypertension
- Healthcare access in underserved communities

### Livelihoods

The Livelihoods program focuses on sustainable economic opportunity for disadvantaged and vulnerable communities. Key areas include:

- Capacity building
- Natural resource management
- Livestock development
- Agriculture and farmer growth
- Community collectives
- Grassroots value chain strengthening
- Farmer market access
- Youth skilling and enterprise promotion
- Productive asset creation
- Fruit orchards and land productivity

## Parent Brand Tone: Lupin

Lupin's corporate LinkedIn tone is purpose-led, polished, healthcare-first, and institutional. The parent company repeatedly uses themes of purpose, care, access, quality, trust, patient outcomes, and responsible progress.

Lupin's public LinkedIn tagline is:

> There is only one reason why we exist - to treat diseases, to heal and enrich human life.

For LHWRF, the parent tone should act as a credibility base, but the foundation should not sound exactly like a pharmaceutical corporate page. LHWRF needs a more field-rooted voice.

Recommended LHWRF tone:

**Warm, human, field-led, dignified, and evidence-backed.**

The tone should begin with real people and field realities, then connect those stories to measurable change, implementation credibility, and long-term systems thinking.

## What LHWRF Wants From An Agency

LHWRF has issued an RFP for appointment of a Social Media Management Agency. The stated deadline is 10 May 2026.

The agency is expected to manage LHWRF's social media presence across:

- Facebook
- LinkedIn
- Instagram
- Twitter/X
- YouTube

The RFP indicates that LHWRF is not merely looking for post design or caption writing. It is looking for a professional agency that can convert field impact into a credible, visible, multi-platform communication system.

## Stated Objectives From The RFP

LHWRF wants the agency to:

- Build strong brand visibility for LHWRF's initiatives
- Create compelling content showcasing impact stories from grassroots communities
- Amplify farmer success stories and healthcare interventions
- Engage supporters, partners, beneficiaries, and the development sector community
- Manage LHWRF's social media presence across multiple platforms

## Scope Of Work

### Social Media Strategy And Management

The selected agency must:

- Develop a comprehensive social media strategy aligned with LHWRF's mission
- Manage Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, and YouTube
- Create and maintain a content calendar
- Deliver a minimum of 18-22 posts per month
- Monitor, moderate, and respond to comments or queries within 24 hours

### Content Creation And Storytelling

The selected agency must create:

- Impact stories
- Beneficiary testimonials
- Case studies
- Graphics
- Short videos of 30-90 seconds
- Infographics
- Reels
- Field documentation content from program locations
- Campaign creatives for key initiatives

The RFP specifies a minimum of two campaign creatives per quarter.

The agency should also be able to translate content into Marathi, Hindi, and regional languages as needed.

### Reporting And Analytics

The selected agency must provide:

- Monthly performance reports
- Engagement metrics
- Reach analysis
- Follower growth analysis
- Impact indicators
- Quarterly strategy reviews and recommendations

## Eligibility And Preference Signals

The RFP requires:

- Minimum three years of experience in social media management
- At least two projects in development, CSR, NGO, or social sector communication
- In-house English and Hindi content writers
- Graphic designers
- Video editors
- Valid PAN and GST registration

Preferred qualifications include:

- Agriculture communication experience
- Rural development communication experience
- Healthcare communication experience
- Portfolio of impact storytelling and beneficiary-centric content
- Understanding of development sector terminology and CSR frameworks

## What This Means Strategically

LHWRF already has strong raw material:

- Long operational history
- Parent company credibility
- Large rural footprint
- Lives and Livelihoods program architecture
- Field stories across farmers, women, youth, health, water, natural resources, and community institutions
- Recognition and implementation credibility

The communications gap is likely not a lack of impact. The gap is packaging.

The agency's role should be framed as:

**Moving LHWRF from activity updates to field-story-led impact storytelling.**

## Recommended Strategic Positioning

**LHWRF transforms rural communities by connecting livelihoods, health, women's agency, and community institutions into long-term family-level change.**

This positioning is strong because it directly reflects LHWRF's own program logic:

- Lives plus Livelihoods
- Family-centered poverty reduction
- Community-level development
- Sustainable economic opportunity
- Healthcare access
- Partnerships and implementation scale

## LinkedIn Strategy

LinkedIn should be treated as LHWRF's credibility platform.

The purpose of LinkedIn should be:

**Turn grassroots work into partnership-worthy proof.**

The platform should speak primarily to:

- CSR leaders
- Funders
- Development-sector partners
- Women empowerment organisations
- Government and institutional stakeholders
- NGO and social impact professionals
- Potential employees and collaborators
- Individuals interested in rural change

LinkedIn should not become only an emotional storytelling channel. It should combine human field stories with implementation logic and evidence.

## LinkedIn Content Territory

Recommended central territory:

**Human stories that prove rural transformation works when health, livelihoods, women's agency, and community ownership move together.**

Every strong LHWRF LinkedIn post should:

1. Start with a person or field situation
2. Reveal the system behind the change
3. Show why the intervention matters
4. End with proof, learning, or partnership relevance

## Recommended Content Pillars

### 1. Field Stories With Proof

Stories of farmers, women, youth, patients, and community members whose lives changed through LHWRF interventions.

Use for:

- Beneficiary stories
- Farmer success stories
- Women-led livelihood stories
- Health access stories
- Before-and-after field narratives

### 2. Lives Plus Livelihoods

Explain the connection between health, income, productivity, dignity, and family resilience.

Use for:

- COPD, CVD, asthma, and hypertension awareness
- Healthcare access
- Rural household resilience
- Why health is an economic issue in rural communities

### 3. Women-Led Rural Change

Show how women are central to household resilience, livelihood improvement, health awareness, enterprise, and community participation.

Use for:

- SHG stories
- Women farmers
- Women entrepreneurs
- Nutrition and health champions
- Local leadership narratives

### 4. Farmer And Livelihood Transformation

Show how LHWRF supports sustainable income through agriculture, livestock, natural resource management, market access, and value chains.

Use for:

- Agriculture stories
- Livestock development
- Better Cotton Initiative work
- Farmer collectives
- Soil, water, and productivity stories

### 5. Implementation Credibility

Content that builds confidence among CSR leaders, funders, and development partners.

Use for:

- Awards
- Ratings
- Government partnerships
- MoUs
- Field scale
- Program milestones
- Impact assessment findings
- Process stories from the field

### 6. Field Notes From Teams

Short, grounded observations from field staff and program teams.

Use for:

- Learnings from villages
- What field teams are seeing
- Implementation challenges
- Community participation insights
- Local language and cultural nuance

## Central Brand Identity Idea

**The Family Is The Unit Of Change**

This should be the central brand identity idea for LHWRF.

It means LHWRF should be known for one distinctive belief:

**Rural development only lasts when the whole family's reality changes, not when one isolated problem is solved.**

Most organisations talk about sectors: health, agriculture, women, youth, livelihoods, water, skilling.

LHWRF can stand for a bigger view:

**In rural India, these are not separate issues. They all meet inside one family.**

A health problem affects income.
Unstable income affects nutrition.
Poor land productivity affects education.
Women's agency affects household decisions.
Market access affects whether farming becomes viable.
Community support affects whether change lasts.

So LHWRF does not just work on "health" or "livelihoods."
It works on the **family system**.

LHWRF strengthens rural families as complete systems by improving the conditions that help them live healthier, earn better, participate more fully, and move forward with dignity.

### Brand Idea

**The Family Is The Unit Of Change**

### Belief

Rural transformation begins when a family becomes healthier, more economically secure, more informed, and better supported by its community.

### Role Of LHWRF

To build the health, livelihood, skill, resource, and community systems that make this change possible.

### What LHWRF Should Be Known For

Not charity. Not isolated interventions.

**Family-centered rural transformation.**

This can shape everything:

- The visual identity can revolve around the family as the center, with systems around it.
- The storytelling can always show how one intervention changes household reality.
- Reports can measure change at family and community level.
- LinkedIn can explain how different sectors connect inside rural life.
- Films can follow families, not just programs.
- The content architecture can include Lives, Livelihoods, Women, Farmers, Youth, and Community, all returning to family progress.

If someone asks, "What does LHWRF stand for?"

The answer becomes:

**LHWRF believes the family is the real unit of rural change. Its work strengthens the systems around rural families so progress can last.**

## Agency Proposal Angle

The strongest agency pitch should be:

**We will help LHWRF become the most credible field-story-led rural transformation voice among CSR foundations in India.**

The pitch should show that the agency understands both sides of LHWRF's need:

- Human storytelling from the grassroots
- Institutional credibility for CSR and development-sector stakeholders

The proposal should avoid sounding like a generic social media retainer. It should demonstrate a repeatable system for:

- Identifying stories from the field
- Turning stories into platform-native content
- Creating monthly calendars
- Producing short video and graphic assets
- Reporting performance
- Reviewing strategy quarterly
- Building brand visibility around real impact
