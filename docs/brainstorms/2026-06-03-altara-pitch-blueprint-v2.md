# Altara Pitch Blueprint v2

**Use this for frontend-slides, not the old slide copy.**  
The deck should feel like a film treatment, rather than a strategy document pasted onto slides.

## Design Guidelines

- Keep the vermilion slides incredibly clean. Avoid using the oversized monogram.
- Steer clear of fake-looking graphs, chunky arcs, and flowcharts.
- Rely on chapter cards, split layouts, contact sheets, film stills, thin rules, and restrained image placeholders.
- Focus on one clear thought per slide. If a slide requires more than two short lines, split it into two.
- Strictly use Altara's design system: warm paper backgrounds, vermilion for punctuation, Ivy Presto for headlines, and Roboto for overlines. Use hairline rules and only use arch frames when they naturally frame an image, not as diagrams.
- Reference examples should act as moodboard slides after the core idea is clear, rather than cluttering every individual slide.
