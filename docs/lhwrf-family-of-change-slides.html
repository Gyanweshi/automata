02

Meet Lata

Start with one home.

Lata lives in a village with her family. Her husband farms. Some months are steady, some are not.

Her children's needs depend on what the household can manage that season. Her life does not fit into one neat category.

03

One problem rarely stays as one problem

A health expense can disturb the whole household.
A weak crop can affect what the children need.
If income slips, nutrition slips.
If Lata is not part of decisions, many choices stay smaller than they need to be.
04

This is rural life

Everything touches everything.

A health problem affects income.
Income affects food.
Land affects education.
Women's agency affects household decisions.
Community support affects whether change survives the season.
05

The LHWRF insight

LHWRF works at the place where health, livelihoods, agriculture, and women's participation meet.

The family.

06

The family is bigger than one home

In a village, support has many faces.

The women's group The farmer collective The field worker The neighbour who shares information The community that keeps showing up The systems around the home
07

Health protects the family

Care protects more than health.

For Lata's family, healthcare access does not only mean treatment. It means less fear of sudden shocks.

A screening, a diagnosis, a follow-up, or timely care can protect income, time, confidence, and household stability.

08

Livelihoods give the family room to plan

Income changes the horizon.

For Lata's family, livelihood support does not only mean more income. It means more room to plan.

When farming support, livestock, skills, or market access improve, the family can think beyond the next crisis.

09

Women change the direction of the household

When Lata has more say, the family has more ways forward.

Her participation can affect savings, nutrition, enterprise, health awareness, children's needs, and the confidence to try something new.

Women's agency is not a side story. It is central to the family's progress.

10

Community helps change last

Progress becomes stronger when it is not carried alone.

SHGs
Farmer groups

11

This is The Family Of Change

No single intervention creates rural transformation.

Change lasts when many forms of support come together around a family. That is the story LHWRF is already living on the ground.

12

The communication shift

From: "LHWRF conducted a program."
To: "Here is what became stronger around Lata's family."

This moves LHWRF from activity updates to family-first impact storytelling.

13

How this becomes social content

Ask one question every time.

What changed around the family?

Health access Livelihood security Women's agency Farming resilience Youth opportunity Community support
14

Why this can hold the communication together

LHWRF has many stories to tell: farmer progress, healthcare access, women's participation, field teams, community institutions, regional voices, and partnerships.

The Family Of Change gives all of them one centre.

15

Closing thought

Lata's story is not about one intervention.

It is about many forms of support coming together until a family can move forward. And when many families move forward, the village begins to change too.

That is the role LHWRF can become known for.
